# Clinical source ladder

Use the highest applicable level and cross-check consequential decisions.

## Japan-first operational sources

1. PMDA electronic package insert, interview form, safety communication, and recall information: https://www.pmda.go.jp/
2. Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare notices and guidance: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/
3. Current Japanese specialty-society guidance, including JCS and JHRS when relevant.
4. The user's institutional protocol, formulary, pharmacy compatibility database, and device instructions.

## Evidence synthesis

1. Current guideline from a recognized professional body with disclosed methods and conflicts.
2. Systematic review or meta-analysis with population and outcome matching the question.
3. Randomized trial for efficacy and comparative harm.
4. High-quality observational study for uncommon harms, prognosis, and real-world effects.
5. Mechanistic, animal, in-vitro, case-series, or expert material only when stronger evidence is absent.

Useful search entry points:

- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
- PubMed Clinical Queries: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinical/
- Cochrane Library: https://www.cochranelibrary.com/
- NICE guidance: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance

## Minimum extraction fields

- Source and update date
- Population and exclusions
- Intervention/exposure and comparator
- Absolute and relative outcomes when available
- Follow-up duration
- Adverse events and interactions
- Applicability to the current patient or Japanese practice
- Major limitations and unresolved uncertainty
