# ECG teaching and review rubric

## Level 1 domains

Cover progressively:

- sinus rhythms, atrial rhythms, junctional rhythms, ventricular rhythms;
- premature beats, escape beats, fusion and capture;
- narrow- and wide-complex tachycardia mechanisms;
- AV block, bundle and fascicular block, aberrancy, pacing;
- pre-excitation and inherited arrhythmia patterns;
- ischemia, infarction, reciprocal change, and mimics;
- hypertrophy, cardiomyopathy, electrolyte, drug, temperature, and systemic effects;
- pediatric/congenital and postoperative patterns when within the official scope;
- artifacts and lead misplacement.

Use the current JHRS ECG Certification page to verify scope and logistics: https://new.jhrs.or.jp/recognition/kentei/

## Deliberate-practice loop

1. Present one tracing or focused question.
2. Require a structured answer, not only a label.
3. Score technical setup, rhythm logic, measurements, morphology, differential, and urgency separately.
4. Turn each error into one corrective card and one near-neighbor comparison.
5. Re-test the same distinction with a new, original example after a delay.

## Spaced repetition

- Prefer short daily reviews over rare long sessions.
- Use FSRS when Anki supports it and let review history optimize the parameters rather than manually guessing them.
- Set desired retention by balancing exam date and daily review burden.
- Avoid multi-fact cards and screenshots whose answer depends on unreadably small details.

Official Anki FSRS guidance: https://docs.ankiweb.net/deck-options.html#fsrs

## Quality checks for generated questions

- Ensure one defensible best answer.
- Include enough calibration and lead context.
- Make distractors plausible but not dependent on hidden facts.
- Explain why every option is right or wrong.
- Mark any criterion that varies across guidelines or editions.
