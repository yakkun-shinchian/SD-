# Full wrap paperback cover workflow

## Freeze the interior first

Do not finalize a cover from an estimated page count. Use the final interior PDF page count, including front matter, back matter, and intentional blanks. If the count is odd, account for KDP's effective even count before calculating the spine.

## Verify current official inputs

Immediately before export, verify on official KDP pages:

- trim size;
- ink and paper type;
- final page count;
- required cover bleed;
- current spine coefficient or generated template;
- minimum page count and usable width for spine text;
- barcode and safe-zone rules.

Use the official KDP cover calculator/template as the final geometry authority. The bundled calculator is a planning and cross-check tool.

## Build one continuous page

From left to right for a left-to-right book:

1. outer bleed;
2. back cover;
3. spine;
4. front cover;
5. outer bleed.

Cover height is top bleed + trim height + bottom bleed. Extend every background through bleed. Do not place independent front and back pages into the cover upload.

## Design the panels

### Front cover

- Preserve the Kindle edition's recognizable title and visual identity.
- Recompose for the physical trim and spine rather than stretching the ebook JPEG.
- Keep title, subtitle, author, and critical faces inside safe zones.

### Spine

- Center the title and author optically, not only mathematically.
- Keep text clear of both fold-variance zones.
- Omit spine text when the current page-count rule or usable width makes it unsafe.

### Back cover

- Use a concise description, imprint/author mark, and optional series information.
- Reserve a clean barcode zone when KDP will add the barcode. Do not place text or important art under it.
- Keep contrast high enough for small printed text.

## Validate the final cover

- exactly one PDF page;
- exact template dimensions;
- all fonts embedded;
- backgrounds reach every bleed edge;
- no template guides remain in the upload PDF;
- no transparency, clipping, or low-resolution warning;
- fold lines do not cross letters or faces;
- page count and paper type match the frozen interior and KDP setup.

After any page-count change, invalidate the old cover and rebuild its spine geometry.
