# Paperback delivery quality gates

## Interior gate

- PDF opens without a password and contains at least the current minimum page count.
- All pages have one consistent size and the selected bleed model.
- Every meaningful font is embedded.
- Image resolution is reviewed at placed size.
- Page 1 and all chapter starts land on the intended side.
- Every repeated image/caption pair is checked in a spread preview.
- Page numbers are sequential after blank-page changes.
- Copyright and colophon say paperback/print edition where appropriate.
- No KDP proof label, grey surround, crop-mark scaffold, or preview-only page remains.

## Cover gate

- Cover was calculated after the final interior freeze.
- One continuous back-spine-front PDF page is supplied.
- Trim, bleed, paper preset, effective page count, and spine coefficient are recorded.
- Official KDP template dimensions match the cover PDF.
- Barcode area is clear or contains the user's authorized barcode.
- Spine text is permitted and has enough fold clearance, or is intentionally omitted.
- Fonts are embedded and important elements are inside safe zones.

## Cross-file gate

- Title, subtitle, author, edition, language, and imprint agree across files and KDP fields.
- Interior page count equals the count used for the spine and pricing calculation.
- Print color choice matches the artwork and cost decision.
- AI-generated-content disclosure matches the production record.
- The latest KDP preview is reviewed after the latest upload.

## Handoff gate

Deliver individual upload files and label the spread preview as review-only. Record:

- final interior filename and page count;
- final cover filename and dimensions;
- trim, bleed, binding direction, ink/paper, and finish;
- spine width and whether spine text is present;
- barcode responsibility;
- remaining KDP warnings, if any;
- proof-copy recommendation.
