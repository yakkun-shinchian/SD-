## Common closing page

End the edition with five shared tools:

1. `事実`: date, place, exact words, action, and patient impact.
2. `復唱`: priority, role, decision owner, and report trigger.
3. `記録`: repeated conduct recorded without personality judgments.
4. `相談`: preceptor, leader, manager, or formal consultation route.
5. `安全`: urgent change, ambiguous direction, and procedural deviation override fear of another person's mood.

Suggested closing line: `あなたの価値は、誰かの不機嫌では決まりません。`
