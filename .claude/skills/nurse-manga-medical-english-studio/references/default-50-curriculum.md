# Default 50-lesson curriculum

Use this sequence when no specialty or custom curriculum is supplied. Keep the progression but adapt vocabulary and scenes to the selected clinical area. The tier is a design flag, not a legal classification.

## Part 1 — Greeting, identity, and orientation

| No. | Scene and goal | Reusable language frame | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Greet the patient and introduce the nurse role | Hello, I'm ... / I'll be your nurse ... | routine |
| 2 | Confirm full name and date of birth | Could you tell me your ...? | caution |
| 3 | Ask what the patient prefers to be called | What would you like me to call you? | routine |
| 4 | Show the room and call button | This is ... / Press this button if ... | caution |
| 5 | State the purpose and ask permission | I'm here to ... / Is it okay if I ...? | routine |
| 6 | Protect privacy before care | I'd like to close the curtain. | routine |
| 7 | Check hearing, vision, and communication needs | Do you use ...? / What helps you ...? | caution |
| 8 | Ask for repetition or slower speech | Could you say that again? | routine |
| 9 | Confirm what the nurse heard | Let me make sure I heard you correctly. | caution |
| 10 | Explain the need for qualified language support | I want to make sure we understand each other clearly. | high |

| 12 | Position the arm for blood pressure | Could you relax your arm? | routine |
| 13 | Check oxygen level and breathing | Are you having any trouble breathing? | caution |
| 14 | Ask where pain is located | Where does it hurt? / Could you point to it? | caution |

| 17 | Ask about nausea and vomiting | Do you feel nauseated? / Have you vomited? | caution |
| 18 | Ask about dizziness before mobilizing | Do you feel dizzy or light-headed? | caution |
| 19 | Assess safe mobility and offer help | How do you usually get around? | caution |
| 20 | Offer toileting help and preserve dignity | Do you need to use the restroom? | routine |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 | Explain a blood draw | I need to draw some blood. | caution |
| 22 | Explain an IV insertion without promising no pain | You'll feel a quick pinch. | caution |
| 23 | Check an IV site and ask about discomfort | Does it hurt, burn, or feel swollen here? | caution |

| No. | Scene and goal | Reusable language frame | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 31 | Ask the patient to call before standing | Please call us before you get up. | caution |
| 32 | Give one-step instructions during a transfer | On three, please stand with me. | caution |
| 33 | Respond safely to chest pain | Stay still. I'm getting help now. | high |
| 34 | Respond to sudden weakness or speech change | When did this start? I'm calling the doctor now. | high |
| 35 | Respond to breathing difficulty | I'm getting help and checking your oxygen now. | high |
| 36 | Ask about fever and chills, then escalate appropriately | Do you feel feverish or have chills? | high |
| 37 | Explain hand hygiene and cough etiquette | Please clean your hands before ... | routine |

## Part 5 — Discharge, self-management, Teach-Back, and interpreter handoff

| No. | Scene and goal | Reusable language frame | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 41 | Confirm discharge support and transportation | Who will be with you at home? | caution |
| 42 | Review a medicine schedule without changing orders | When will you take this medicine? | caution |
| 43 | Explain warning signs and where to seek help | Please get help right away if ... | high |
| 44 | Review basic wound care | Keep the area clean and dry as instructed. | caution |
| 45 | Review diet and fluid instructions | What will you eat and drink at home? | caution |
| 46 | Review activity limits and follow-up | When is your follow-up appointment? | caution |
| 47 | Use non-shaming Teach-Back for the medication plan | To make sure I explained it clearly, please tell me ... | caution |
| 48 | Use Show-Me for a practical self-care task | Could you show me how you'll ...? | caution |
| 49 | Offer written material and qualified language support | We can provide this information with language support. | high |
| 50 | Recognize limits and hand off an important explanation | This is important. I'll arrange an interpreter and contact the doctor. | high |

## Spaced-repetition rule

Recur intentionally across different parts:

- Could you ...? in identity, positioning, symptom assessment, and Show-Me
- Let me ... in clarification, assessment, and handoff
- I'm going to ... in vital signs, procedures, and urgent action
- Please let me know if ... in symptoms, IV care, and discharge
- I want to make sure ... in clarification, Teach-Back, and interpreter use

Keep the frame recognizable while changing the clinical task, vocabulary, response, and visual action.
