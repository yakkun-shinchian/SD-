## Behavioral evidence anchors

- Implementation intentions translate goals into cue-linked action: https://cancercontrol.cancer.gov/brp/research/constructs/implementation-intentions
- Meta-analysis reference: Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006), DOI 10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1
- CFPB financial empowerment materials: https://www.cfpb.gov/your-money-your-goals/

Use these as process evidence, not as a promise that a plan will work without adaptation.
